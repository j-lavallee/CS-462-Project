
---Demo Intro---

Main Character: MC
Internal Dialogue: ID
Shopkeeper: S


(Pop-up box describing the story so far, at the opening of the demo at the general store)

"All the coins in the town fountain have been stolen! They were supposed to go to a charity! 
Luckily, you've investigated the fountain and found a clue: some chocolate fingerprints and a candy wrapper from the general store.
See if you can find out where it came from and look for more clues!"
  
---MC-Shopkeeper Intro Conversation Dialogue---
  

ID: The general store shopkeeper, Mr. S. He looks busy. Well, he looks more worried than working.

MC: "Hello Mr. S!"

S: "HUH? OH! Hi, yes, um, hello. I mean, um, how may I help you? "

MC: "I'm looking for clues! About the fountain coin theft!"

S: "Oh, the fountain? Wait, theft? What theft? . . . OH! Oh yes, the fountain! Terrible news, um, very terrible. The fountain. Coins. Yep. 
  Hope someone finds it soon. . . I mean them! The coins! Find them too!"

MC: "Um, exactly! I even found a clue! This candy wrapper!"

S: "Huh, you looking for more candy? Sorry, but I can't clear this isle until I've restocked the shelves. If I could find it already. . ."

MC: "I'm not looking for more candy, I'm looking for who bought this one. I'm trying to investigate!"

S: "Investigate! Yes, thats right, I just need to, um, look harder. Then I can continue!"

ID: It seems like I won't be able to ask Mr. S anything while he's so distracted. If only grownups would take me more seriously! This is a serious investigation!

MC: "Well, What are you looking for Mr. S?"

S: "Oh dear, it's my store receipts! A whole stack of them! I don't know how I misplaced it. . . again!"

MC: "Maybe I can find them for you?" 

S: "Oh, I'm not sure about that. Sensitive information, on receipts! Here, if you would be so kind, could you perhaps help me stock these last few items? 
  Then I can return to searching without being behind!" 

MC: "Sure, I can do that." 

S: "Thank you! When you're finished you can see me at the counter and I'll get you some candy."

ID: I got the stock items; maybe I can look around the store for more clues while I'm at it!

---Restock Quest---

(Upon interacting with a prompt to restock)
ID: There! In it's right place!

(Upon interacting with a red herring spot)
ID: Hmm. . . nothing stands out. I don't think there's anything important here. . .

(Upon Interacting with a Clue)
ID: What's that?
ID: [Clue Dialogue]

If finished restocking before clues:
ID: Ok that's done! I should poke around a bit for more clues while I'm here.

If finished clues before restocking:
ID: I think I've found everything worth investigating, I should finish Mr. S's task.

If finished both:
ID: I think that's everything! Now to find Mr. S. . . 

---Stealth Section---

(Upon being spotted)
S:"Hey! Sorry, um, but you're not supposed to be back here. I'll be out soon, alright?"

(Fade out to teleport outside the employee's only entrance)
ID: Darn it! I'll have to be sneakier! Like a real detective!

(Upon retrieving the receipt stack)
ID: Ooh! A stack of papers! Let's see. . . it's got a bunch of numbers on it. . . is the stack of receipts Mr. S was looking for? They were right here this whole time! I'd better return it to him.

---Clues---

Wet Coin In the Tip Jar:
ID: Ew, the tip jar is kinda wet. . . Hey wait a minute! One of the coins in here looks really old! I think it's from the fountain!
The thief must have bought something here for sure! But why would a stolen coin get left behind?


Scrap Costume Fabric: 
ID: Weird, there's a large piece of cloth left here. It looks like a cape or something? Not sure if it's important now, but I'd better mark it as a clue just in case. 

Crumpled Up Receipt:
ID: The top receipt is a little wrinkled, and is stained with chocolate. Let's see. . . two pairs of black gloves and hats? A flashlight? AND some candy bars? 
This looks like something the theif would have bought! Or, no! There's more than one thief! 

ID: Wait a minute! There's something else here!

Library Page Treasure Map:
ID: It looks like someone drew a map? And at the end is a pile of coins! This must have been used to plan the theft.

ID: There's also a mark here. This paper is from the library? . . . Of course! That's where the thieves must have planned the coin theft. I need to go there and investigate immediately!

---MC-Shopkeep Receipt Return Conversation---
MC: Mr. S! I have something to tell you!

S: Oh! Um, you finished restocking those shelves already? Thank you!

MC: No that's not it! I mean, yes I finished the shelves, but there's something else!

S: Oh right you wanted candy, I have some right here--

MC: I FOUND IT! 

S: Huh? Found what?

MC: YOUR RECEIPTS!

S: Goodness, there's no need to shout-- wait, you actually found them!? Thank you, oh thank you! I'm so relieved! Here, um, you've definitely earned this now!
*Gives candy* (just some flavor text)

MC: Thank you! I'm off to look for more clues now. Bye!

S: Take care! Good luck on the, um, investigation!

ID: Huh, maybe he did remember what I was saying. . . Anyways, I'm off!
