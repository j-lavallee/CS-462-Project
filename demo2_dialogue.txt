---Library Sequence Section-----
Flag References:
Entry and NPC
LIB_ENTERED: player crosses the entrance trigger (plays once).
TEAL_MET: intro conversation finished. Unlocks all interactables.
MAP_SHOWN: map page shown to Miss Teal (set together with TEAL_MET).
Prep jobs
JOB1_BANNER_DONE: banner hung.
CUSHIONS_PLACED (0–3): increments per cushion arranged.
JOB2_CUSHIONS_DONE: counter reaches 3.
JOB3_UNLOCKED: set the moment both job 1 and job 2 are done, whichever finishes second.
JOB3_SIGN_DONE: sign-swap conversation finished.
Clue 1: Coin History Book
HIGHBOOK_SPOTTED: player looks at the out-of-place book for the first time from in front of the shelf.
LADDER_MOVED: ladder rolled to the correct shelf.
CLUE_HISTORY_FOUND: book collected and clue dialogue finished.
Clue 2: Stamped Picture Book
BELT_JAM_SEEN: player has inspected the jammed belt and seen the coin.
SCISSORS_HELD: scissors taken from the craft chest.
BELT_COIN_REMOVED: coin extracted, belt restarts.
CLUE_PICTUREBOOK_FOUND: book collected and clue dialogue finished.
Clue 3: Jigsaw Note
PIECE_1_REVEALED (set by job 1), PIECE_2_REVEALED (set when the hiding cushion is lifted), PIECE_3_SPOTTED (interact with armchair). Piece 4 is visible from the start.
PIECE_1_FOUND … PIECE_4_FOUND, plus PIECES_FOUND (0–4): picked up.
PIECES_PLACED (0–4): pieces added to the table.
JIGSAW_TABLE_SEEN: first interaction with the puzzle table.
JIGSAW_COMPLETE: minigame solved.
CLUE_JIGSAWNOTE_FOUND: note read.
Overall progress
CLUES_FOUND (0–3): increments on each of the three clue discoveries.
DEDUCTION_PLAYED: scene 8 monologue played.
READY_FOR_RETURN: JOB1_BANNER_DONE + JOB2_CUSHIONS_DONE + JOB3_SIGN_DONE all true AND CLUES_FOUND = 3.
RETURN_CONVO_DONE, NOTE_RECEIVED, SCHOOLHOUSE_UNLOCKED: end-of-section flags.

Fixed-Order Dependencies:
Jigsaw Piece 1 REQUIRES Job 1 (banner)
Jigsaw Piece 2 REQUIRES Job 2 (cushion hiding it lifted)
Jigsaw Piece 3 REQUIRES Player investigates armchair
Clue 3 (jigsaw puzzle) REQUIRES All 4 pieces, meaning jobs 1 & 2 must be done
Clue 1 (history book) REQUIRES ladder in position
Clue 2 (picture book) REQUIRES Scissors from craft chest
Job 3 (sign swap) REQUIRES Jobs 1 & 2
Return conversation REQUIRES Job 3 + all 3 clues

All else can happen in any order
